DCT and PCA denoisers built from scratch and measured across three subjects and four b-values. PCA wins by ~3.5 dB — but both hit the same wall, and the trade-off curve is the real finding.

The weakest model made the best ensemble

LeNet, AlexNet, VGG and ResNet stacked under a meta-network on CIFAR-10. The winning trio included the 68% model — with a live stacking playground that shows why diversity beats strength.

Research paper · Dec 2022

Finding a tumour with no labels

Unsupervised brain-tumour segmentation on BraTS 2018 — k-means against a GMM-EM written from scratch in PyTorch and run on the GPU over nine million voxels. Dice 0.9 on most patients, and a precise account of the ones it missed.

About

I'm a software engineer based in the Vancouver area. Most of my work sits between a domain problem and the code that has to model it — currently geospatial modelling of critical infrastructure networks, and before that retrieval-augmented language model systems.

I like the parts other people find tedious: reading the paper properly, finding the one assumption everything rests on, and getting a system to the point where the hard thing looks obvious. This site is mostly a record of that.

M.Eng. from the University of British Columbia, B.Sc. (Honours) from the University of Victoria.

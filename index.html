Graduate research projects

Applied machine learning and signal processing from the M.Eng. at UBC, 2022 – 2024 — the reasoning, not just the screenshot.

Research paper · Apr 2024

Catching AI images by their missing mosaic

A camera interpolates two of the three colours at every pixel; a generator never does. Re-mosaic an image four ways and measure the disagreement: 97–100% detection across 46,000 generated images with no training — and a live version you can feed your own photo.

Interactive Image forensics Signal processing PyTorch

Research paper · Apr 2024

Distilling face recognition across an age gap

A ResNet-100 teacher distilled into age-invariant students, trained as SLURM jobs on Compute Canada. The distillation transferred a 19-point gain — and still lost to the specialist. Both results, and why.

Interactive Knowledge distillation HPC / SLURM PyTorch

Research report · Dec 2023

Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models

A full derivation of the DDPM training objective, from maximum likelihood to the one-line noise-prediction loss — rebuilt as an interactive page with a live forward-process simulator.

Interactive Variational inference Generative models LaTeX

Research report · May 2023

The weakest model made the best ensemble

LeNet, AlexNet, VGG and ResNet stacked under a meta-network on CIFAR-10. The winning trio included the 68% model — with a live stacking playground that shows why diversity beats strength.

Interactive Ensembles PyTorch CIFAR-10

Solo research project · Apr 2023

Feature extractors for few-shot learning

ResNet, DenseNet and ConvNeXt trained from scratch as backbones for Prototypical Networks on CUB — plus the two classifier improvements that didn't work, and why.

Interactive PyTorch Meta-learning Ablation

Research paper · Dec 2022

Denoising diffusion-weighted MRI

DCT and PCA denoisers built from scratch and measured across three subjects and four b-values. PCA wins by ~3.5 dB — but both hit the same wall, and the trade-off curve is the real finding.

Interactive Signal processing Medical imaging NumPy

Research paper · Dec 2022

Finding a tumour with no labels

Unsupervised brain-tumour segmentation on BraTS 2018 — k-means against a GMM-EM written from scratch in PyTorch and run on the GPU over nine million voxels. Dice 0.9 on most patients, and a precise account of the ones it missed.
